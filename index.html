        Goals

        Click any goal in the top-left list to jump to a good spot for it.

        Touch

        Left thumb moves; push far for the afterburner. Right thumb looks. Buttons on screen board trains and look back.

        Flashing lights

        Some places have lightning and fireworks, which flash brightly.

        Coming back

        The address bar keeps your place, position and Lab settings, so a refresh (or a shared link) puts you back where you were.

        Reading the screen

        Your watch runs on your own time. The platform clock runs on the station's. The gap between them is how much younger you are. In the observation log, seen is when an event's light reached you, and happened is when it took place in your frame, with the light's travel time taken out.

        Slowlight

        Light here moves at a walking pace. Everything Einstein said follows, and you can watch it happen.
